I am currently a **PhD Candidate** in **Mechanical Engineering** at the University of California, Los Angeles (**UCLA**). My current research focuses on **digital health**, **wearable haptics**, and **physical human–robot interaction (pHRI)**. I received my B.Eng. in **Robotics** from Beijing University of Technology (**BJUT**) in 2024, and spent my senior year as an exchange student at the National University of Singapore (**NUS**) Suzhou Research Institute.

During my previous studies, I worked on **exoskeleton control**, **quadruped robot path planning**, and **tactile sensors**.

My broader research interests span **adaptive control**, **robot perception**, and **intelligent assistive systems**, with the goal of creating **robotic systems** that can **safely and reliably share the same workspace with humans**.
